# Research data — Canopy AI Visibility aggregates (October 2026)

`canopy-ai-visibility-aggregates-2026-10.csv` — 493 rows of aggregate counts from Copp Oak's October 2026 audit of AI-assistant answers to homeowner requests for local service companies: 20 Southeast US metros × 12 home-service trades × platform (ChatGPT, Gemini, plus a Google Search comparison set), computed from 5,932 captured answers. Columns record, per cell: answers captured, answers naming a company, company mentions, distinct companies, answers offering a booking path, answers mentioning a connector, and answers offering neither.

Headline finding: in 79.1% of 5,788 paired ChatGPT/Gemini answers, the assistant offered no booking path and mentioned no connector of any kind.

The file contains aggregate counts only — no answer-level records and no personal data. Full methods and analysis: Copp Oak Advisors (2026), *Named, Not Booked* (working paper), Zenodo https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23745742. The research summary lives at https://coppoak.com/canopy/research/ai-assistant-booking.
